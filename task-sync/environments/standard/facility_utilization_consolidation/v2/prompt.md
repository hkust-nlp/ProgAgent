Your municipality has tasked you with reviewing its entire portfolio of public facilities to determine which buildings are underutilized and could be candidates for consolidation. You have access to monthly occupancy survey data files that record average and peak daily visitor counts for each facility over various months. A policy configuration file lays out the consolidation parameters, including the occupancy threshold below which a facility should be flagged, the scoring weights and benchmark values used to assess consolidation urgency, and the recommendation thresholds that map a final consolidation score to an action. A separate definitions document describes precisely how each metric and scoring component must be computed, and a data file records every facility's annual operating budget.

Begin by determining each facility's average occupancy rate. For every facility in the system, aggregate the average daily visitor figures across all available survey months and divide by the facility's rated capacity, which you can obtain through the facility lookup functions at your disposal. Any facility whose resulting average occupancy rate falls below the threshold specified in the policy configuration must be flagged for further analysis.

For each flagged facility, retrieve its detailed building profile, all quarterly utility billing records, every maintenance log entry regardless of status, all program attendance session records, and its capital improvement requests. Compute the annual utility cost, annual maintenance cost, total pending capital amount, utility cost per square foot, maintenance burden percentage, and average program attendance rate, all following the exact formulas in the definitions document. Then calculate each scoring component and the overall weighted consolidation score as described, and apply the recommendation thresholds to assign one of three labels: closure, shared_space, or retain_with_review.

Save the complete results to a file named consolidation_report.csv with the following columns in this exact order: facility_id, facility_name, zone, facility_type, capacity, square_footage, avg_occupancy_pct, annual_utility_cost, annual_maintenance_cost, pending_capital_amount, cost_per_sqft, maintenance_burden_pct, program_attendance_pct, consolidation_score, recommendation. Round avg_occupancy_pct, cost_per_sqft, maintenance_burden_pct, and program_attendance_pct to two decimal places. Round annual_utility_cost, annual_maintenance_cost, and pending_capital_amount to two decimal places. Round consolidation_score to one decimal place. Sort the rows by consolidation_score in descending order, breaking ties by facility_id in ascending order.

In your final response, state the total number of facilities flagged for consolidation review and list each one by its facility identifier alongside the assigned recommendation.
