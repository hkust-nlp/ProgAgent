You are conducting a comprehensive periodic eligibility review for every active participant currently enrolled in the regional housing assistance programs. The objective is to evaluate each participant's continued qualification for benefits and determine whether any adjustment to their monthly assistance amount, or outright termination of benefits, is warranted based on the most current data available.

Participant records—including identifiers, property assignments, unit details, geographic region, dwelling size, and program enrollment information—can be retrieved through available query functions, as can each participant's income certification history. The most recent income certification for each participant documents their verified annual gross income, income source, and household composition at the time of filing. Fair market rent figures for each geographic region and unit size are also available through query functions, as are the latest property inspection results showing overall condition scores and deficiency counts.

Several additional categories of information are maintained in organized data files. Current household composition records, which reflect the most up-to-date living situations and may differ from the figures recorded on income certifications, are stored in batch files organized by enrollment cohort. Whenever the current household size on file differs from the figure on the income certification, the current household size must govern all calculations that depend on it. Area median income schedules, broken down by region and household size, are documented in reference files within an AMI data directory and are essential for computing the applicable income ceiling. Utility compliance records capturing each participant's rate of timely utility payments are provided in tabular files organized in a compliance directory. The monthly utility allowance applicable to each combination of region and dwelling size is specified in a schedule within a policy directory, alongside a policy document that sets forth all relevant thresholds and rates. Each participant's current monthly benefit disbursement is recorded in a benefits ledger. Detailed property inspection narratives, organized in batch reports, describe specific deficiencies found during inspections along with their severity classifications—marked as MINOR, MAJOR, or CRITICAL.

To evaluate each participant, first retrieve their record and identify their region, dwelling size, and assigned property. Obtain their most recent income certification to determine their annual gross income. Look up the current household size from the household composition records. Using the area median income for the participant's region and current household size, compute the applicable income ceiling by multiplying the area median income by the income threshold percentage specified in the eligibility policy. If the participant's annual income exceeds this ceiling, the determination is TERMINATE.

Next, consult the utility compliance records for the participant. If their compliance rate falls below the minimum utility compliance rate defined in the eligibility policy, the determination is TERMINATE.

Then examine the property inspection results. Retrieve the overall inspection score for the participant's assigned property, and consult the inspection detail reports to ascertain whether any deficiency affecting that property has been classified as CRITICAL. If the overall score falls below the minimum inspection score threshold from the policy, or if any critical-severity deficiency is present for that property, the determination is SUSPEND.

For participants who pass all of the above checks, compute the revised monthly benefit. The payment standard is the fair market rent for the participant's region and dwelling size. Add to this the monthly utility allowance for that same region and dwelling size from the allowance schedule. The participant's expected contribution is the greater of their annual income multiplied by the rent burden ratio from the policy divided by twelve, or the minimum rent floor specified in the policy. The revised benefit equals the payment standard plus the utility allowance minus the participant's contribution; if this value falls to zero or below, set it to zero and the determination is TERMINATE. Otherwise, compare the revised benefit to the participant's current monthly disbursement from the benefit ledger. If the revised benefit exceeds the current disbursement by more than the adjustment threshold percentage specified in the policy, the determination is ADJUST_UP. If it falls below by more than that threshold, the determination is ADJUST_DOWN. In all remaining cases, the determination is MAINTAIN.
