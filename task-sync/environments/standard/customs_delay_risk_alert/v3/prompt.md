You have been brought in to conduct a comprehensive customs delay risk assessment for all shipments that have not yet completed customs clearance. This includes any shipment currently in transit, sitting at port, or awaiting clearance processing. The goal is to flag those shipments most likely to encounter inspection holds or processing delays, so that customs brokers can be dispatched to intervene before problems escalate.

Several categories of information are at your disposal. Carrier advance manifests have been filed detailing each shipment's special handling requirements and whether all required documentation has been submitted. Supplier compliance reports, organized in batches, record each supplier's compliance tier classification, their historical violation count, and the outcome of their most recent audit. Current port advisory bulletins describe ports experiencing active disruptions and quantify the additional delay each disruption is expected to cause. Trade lane risk assessments flag specific origin-to-destination shipping corridors that carry elevated customs scrutiny, expressed as a risk bonus. A risk scoring configuration file lays out the weights assigned to each risk dimension, the alert and priority thresholds, bracket-based scoring tables for carrier on-time performance, point values for special handling categories and documentation gaps, supplier tier score mappings, violation and audit penalty rules, and the recommended broker actions mapped to each risk dimension. Separately, an inspection override file lists HS codes that attract supplementary inspection beyond their baseline rate, each with a specified bonus.

Four risk dimensions feed into the composite score for every active shipment. Port congestion risk equals the destination port's congestion index plus the additional delay factor from any active advisory affecting that port, capped at one hundred. HS code inspection risk equals the commodity's base inspection rate multiplied by one hundred, plus any override bonus listed for that code, again capped at one hundred. Supplier compliance risk starts from the score mapped to the supplier's compliance tier, adds the configured violation penalty if the supplier's violation count exceeds the violation threshold, and adds the audit penalty corresponding to the supplier's latest audit status, with the total capped at one hundred. Carrier reliability risk begins with the score derived from the carrier's on-time rate using the configured bracket table, adds the risk points associated with the shipment's special handling code, and adds the documentation incomplete penalty if the manifest indicates documentation has not been fully submitted, capped at one hundred. The composite score is the sum of each dimension's raw score multiplied by its configured weight, plus the trade lane risk bonus if the shipment's origin-destination corridor appears in the trade lane assessments, with the final result capped at one hundred. Every risk score must be rounded to exactly two decimal places.

Shipments whose composite score meets or exceeds the alert threshold are considered high risk. Among those, any shipment at or above the upper priority threshold receives a risk level of CRITICAL and a broker priority of URGENT, while those below that upper threshold but still at or above the alert threshold receive a risk level of HIGH and a broker priority of HIGH. The primary risk factor for each flagged shipment is the dimension whose weighted contribution to the composite score is greatest. When two or more dimensions tie for the greatest weighted contribution, select the one appearing earliest in this sequence: port congestion, HS code inspection, supplier compliance, carrier reliability. The recommended action corresponds to the primary risk factor as defined in the configuration.

Save the results as `high_risk_alerts.csv` with columns in this order: `shipment_id`, `carrier_name`, `supplier_name`, `destination_port`, `hs_code`, `risk_score`, `risk_level`, `primary_risk_factor`, `recommended_action`, `broker_priority`. Rows must be sorted by `risk_score` descending, with ties broken by `shipment_id` in ascending lexicographic order. In your final response, state the total number of high-risk shipments you identified and name the shipment with the highest composite risk score together with that score.
