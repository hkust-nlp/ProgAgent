You are responsible for generating a comprehensive price adjustment alert list for our product catalog. The company tracks competitor pricing daily and needs to decide, for every active product, whether to match a competitor's price, hold our current price, or recommend a promotional campaign. Your analysis must combine information from multiple sources: tool-based queries for product details, competitor pricing, supplier costs, and conversion history, as well as configuration and data files that define business rules, competitor classifications, and daily scrape observations.

Begin by identifying all product categories using the available tools, then retrieve every product in each category. Only active products should be included in the final alert list; discontinued items are excluded entirely. For each active product, gather the most recent competitor prices through the appropriate tool, obtain supplier cost quotes, and pull conversion history spanning the number of days specified by the conversion lookback period found in the pricing policy configuration file.

Competitor prices must be filtered before comparison. A file in your environment maps each competitor to a tier designation: A, B, or C. Tier C competitors are excluded from all comparisons. Among the remaining tier A and tier B competitors, only those currently holding the product in stock are eligible. Beyond this, daily scrape observation files record a price flag for each product-competitor combination on each day they were observed. Locate the most recent observation for every product-competitor pair across all available scrape files, and exclude any pair whose most recent flag is either "clearance" or "bundle", since those prices reflect non-standard offers that should not drive our pricing decisions. Prices flagged "normal" or "limited_time" remain eligible.

Once the qualifying competitor set for a product has been determined, find the single lowest price among them. That is the best competitor price for comparison. If no qualifying competitor prices remain after filtering, the recommended action is "hold" with the reason "no qualifying competitor prices". If the best competitor price is greater than or equal to our current price, the action is also "hold" with the reason "price competitive".

When a competitor offers a price strictly below ours, compute the adjusted margin that would result from matching that price. The adjusted margin is calculated as the difference between the best competitor price and the lowest available supplier unit cost for that product, divided by the best competitor price, multiplied by one hundred. The current margin uses the same formula but with our own current price in place of the competitor price. Both margins should be rounded to two decimal places. The minimum acceptable margin for each category is specified in the category configuration file. If the adjusted margin meets or exceeds the category minimum, the recommended action is "match" with the reason "margin preserved after matching". If the adjusted margin falls below the minimum, check the product's conversion trend. The conversion trend is determined by comparing average daily purchases in the recent window against the earlier window within the lookback period. The lookback period length, the split point between the two windows, and the threshold for classifying a trend as declining or improving are all defined in the pricing policy file. If the ratio of recent average to earlier average is below one minus the decline threshold, the trend is "declining"; if above one plus the threshold, it is "improving"; otherwise it is "stable". When the adjusted margin is insufficient, the trend is declining, and the category configuration permits promotions, recommend "promote" with the reason "margin squeeze with declining conversion". In all other insufficient-margin cases, recommend "hold" with the reason "insufficient margin to match".

Save the results as `price_alerts.csv` containing exactly these columns in this order: `product_sku`, `product_name`, `category`, `our_price`, `best_competitor_price`, `best_competitor_name`, `supplier_cost`, `current_margin_pct`, `adjusted_margin_pct`, `conversion_trend`, `action`, `reason`. For products with no qualifying competitors, set `best_competitor_price`, `best_competitor_name`, and `adjusted_margin_pct` to the literal string "N/A". The file should be sorted first by category in ascending alphabetical order, then within each category by price gap descending, where price gap is our price minus the best competitor price (products with "N/A" competitor prices have a gap of zero).

In your final response, state the total number of active products analyzed, the count of "match" recommendations, the count of "hold" recommendations, and the count of "promote" recommendations. Also list the top five products with the largest positive price gap, showing each product's SKU, name, our price, the best competitor price, the gap amount, and the recommended action.
