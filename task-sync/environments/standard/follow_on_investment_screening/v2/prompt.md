Your venture capital firm is preparing its quarterly portfolio review and needs to determine which existing portfolio companies should receive follow-on investment. The goal is to produce a single prioritized outreach list that ranks every eligible company by a weighted composite score, assigns each a priority tier, and saves the result as a deliverable file. The analysis draws on multiple data sources: monthly financial performance accessible through the firm's data tools, cap table records, competitor funding intelligence, internal partner sentiment assessments, and strategic intelligence reports stored as files in your working directory.

Begin by retrieving the complete roster of portfolio companies using the available listing tool, then obtain detailed information for each one, since the listing only provides identifiers and names. For every company you will need its sector and current funding stage. The firm's investment criteria document, a YAML configuration file in your working directory, specifies which funding stages are eligible for follow-on consideration, along with minimum thresholds for average monthly revenue over the most recent three months of available financial data, minimum combined founder and co-founder ownership percentage, and minimum average partner sentiment score. A company must satisfy all four eligibility gates to appear on the outreach list.

Once you have identified eligible companies, compute six factor scores for each. The scoring configuration file, a JSON document also in your working directory, contains the weight each factor carries in the composite, the normalization benchmarks, and precise definitions and formulas for every metric. Revenue growth is the average month-over-month percentage change in revenue across the last three monthly transitions of the available data, meaning you need the final four months of records to derive three successive growth rates, and the resulting average is then normalized against the benchmark ceiling and scaled to a zero-to-one-hundred range. Burn efficiency is based on the average burn multiple over the most recent three months, where each month's burn multiple is the burn rate divided by that month's revenue; if a month's revenue is zero or negative the burn multiple for that month equals the configured maximum benchmark, and the final score maps inversely so that a lower burn multiple yields a higher score. Dilution health reflects the sum of ownership percentages held by all shareholders classified as Founder or Co-Founder on the cap table, normalized against a benchmark ownership figure. Competitive position depends on the total amount raised across all competitor funding rounds in the same sector as the portfolio company, with higher aggregate competitor funding producing a lower score. Partner sentiment is simply the arithmetic mean of every sentiment score recorded for the company, scaled to the zero-to-one-hundred range. The sixth factor, strategic fit, comes exclusively from the firm's intelligence assessment files in your working directory; these files appear in several formats and each contains a strategic fit score on a one-to-ten scale for one or more companies, and you must read all of them to collect every company's value before normalizing it in the same way.

The composite score for each eligible company equals the sum of each factor score multiplied by its corresponding weight from the configuration. Round the composite to two decimal places. Based on the tier thresholds in the investment criteria file, assign each company a recommendation tier: companies at or above the high-priority threshold receive "High Priority", those at or above the medium threshold but below the high threshold receive "Medium Priority", and all others receive "Monitor".

Save the output as `prioritized_outreach.csv` in your working directory. The file must contain exactly these columns in this order: `rank`, `company_name`, `composite_score`, `recommendation_tier`, `revenue_growth_pct`, `burn_multiple`, `founder_ownership_pct`, `avg_partner_sentiment`, `strategic_fit_score`, `sector`. Sort all rows by composite score in descending order; where two companies share the same composite score, break the tie by company name in ascending alphabetical order. The rank column should reflect each row's position in that sorted order, starting from one. Express composite score, revenue growth percentage, burn multiple, founder ownership percentage, and average partner sentiment each to two decimal places. Express the strategic fit score to one decimal place, exactly as found in the intelligence files.

In your final response, state how many companies qualified for the outreach list and then name every company that received a "High Priority" tier along with its composite score, tier label, and sector. If no company reached the high-priority threshold, name the top three companies instead. Confirm that the output file has been written.
