You are an analyst at GreenCharge Network, responsible for evaluating the performance of the company's electric vehicle charging station portfolio. Leadership has requested a thorough assessment to identify underperforming stations that should be considered for relocation, prioritized by a composite scoring methodology, and accompanied by projected revenue impact figures.

Begin by reviewing the configuration files that define the evaluation framework. These contain threshold values for determining underperformance, scoring weights for the composite priority calculation, and parameters for the revenue projection model. Understanding these parameters is essential before any station-level analysis.

For each station in the network, compute the adjusted utilization rate over calendar year 2024. The raw utilization is the ratio of total charging hours delivered by the station to the product of its charger count and the annual operating hours specified in the revenue model configuration. However, the denominator must first be reduced by charger-hours lost to downtime. Maintenance and downtime log files record individual incidents, each specifying the duration in hours and the number of chargers affected; the charger-hours lost for each incident equals the product of those two values. Subtract the cumulative charger-hours lost for a station from the theoretical maximum before dividing. Separately, compute the station's failure rate as the total number of failed sessions divided by total sessions for the year. A station qualifies as an underperformance candidate if its adjusted utilization falls below the utilization threshold in the scoring configuration or its failure rate exceeds the failure rate ceiling defined there.

Once the candidate set is established, calculate each candidate's composite relocation priority score. Four components contribute, each scaled between zero and one and multiplied by its weight from the scoring configuration before summing. The utilization deficit equals the utilization threshold minus the station's adjusted utilization, divided by the threshold, floored at zero. The failure severity equals the station's failure rate minus the failure rate threshold, divided by one minus that threshold, floored at zero. The competitor pressure equals the number of competing charging stations within the configured radius of the candidate, divided by the normalization cap from the configuration, capped at one. For the EV growth deficit, retrieve the projected EV growth rate for the candidate's region from the available forecast data; for any region not represented in the forecast files, compute the year-over-year growth rate by summing all EV registration counts for 2024 and comparing against the 2023 total for that same region, expressing the result as a fractional rate. Then determine the median of all projected growth rates present in the forecast files alone — considering only regions that appear in those files — and compute the deficit as the median minus the station's regional growth rate, divided by the median, floored at zero. Multiply each component by its corresponding weight and sum to obtain the composite score. Only candidates whose composite score reaches or exceeds the minimum threshold in the scoring configuration belong in the final relocation list.

For every station on the final list, project the annual revenue loss attributable to underutilization by multiplying the gap between the benchmark utilization from the revenue model configuration and the station's adjusted utilization by the station's charger count, the annual operating hours, and the revenue per charger-hour. Also determine each station's total annual operating cost by summing the four monthly cost components found in the cost data files — lease, electricity, maintenance, and staff — and multiplying by twelve.

Save the results as `relocation_list.csv` with columns in this exact order: `station_id`, `station_name`, `region_name`, `adjusted_utilization`, `failure_rate`, `competitor_count`, `ev_growth_rate`, `composite_score`, `projected_annual_revenue_loss`, `annual_operating_cost`. Express utilization rates, failure rates, growth rates, and composite scores to exactly four decimal places. Express revenue loss and operating cost to exactly two decimal places. Sort rows by composite score in descending order, breaking ties by station identifier in ascending order.

In your final response, state the total number of stations flagged for relocation, report the aggregate projected annual revenue loss across all flagged stations rounded to two decimal places, and list the top three stations by composite score giving each station's identifier, station name, and composite score to four decimal places.
