Your organization operates a chain of retail stores across multiple regions, and leadership has tasked you with producing a prioritized list of underperforming stores that should be considered for closure. The analysis must integrate four key dimensions: point-of-sale revenue trends over time, shifts in local competitor density, lease renewal cost projections, and the efficiency of regional marketing expenditures. Every store in the network must be evaluated; none may be excluded from consideration.

Begin by familiarizing yourself with the configuration files in your working directory. A scoring policy file defines the exact weights, thresholds, and tier mappings used to evaluate each store across the four dimensions, and a separate text file describes the methodology for computing estimated annual cost savings. Regional benchmark data is also available for reference. All decision parameters, including scoring weights, tier boundaries, and adjustment factors, are specified in these configuration files and must be followed exactly as documented.

Revenue trend assessment requires comparing each store's total net revenue over the most recent twelve-month period against the preceding twelve months, expressed as a percentage change. The scoring policy maps this percentage to a tier-based score according to the severity of decline or the presence of growth. Every store must have its revenue trend calculated to determine its score for this dimension.

Competitor pressure for each store is determined by counting newly appeared competitors within a defined radius during a specified observation window, considering only those that remain active. The scoring policy specifies the radius, the window boundaries, and the score tiers based on the count of such new entrants. Competitors that have closed are excluded from this count.

Lease renewal cost impact is gauged by the percentage increase between a store's current monthly rent and the proposed renewal rent. The scoring policy provides base score tiers for different ranges of increase, as well as a special tier when the proposed rent represents a decrease. These base scores are then adjusted using landlord flexibility ratings, which can be found alongside per-store operational cost data in the CSV data files distributed across your working directory. A higher flexibility score indicates that the proposed increase may be negotiable, and the policy defines exact discount factors to apply to the base lease score depending on the flexibility level.

Marketing spend efficiency is evaluated at the regional level for the evaluation year specified in the policy. For each region, the ratio of total attributed revenue to total marketing spend determines the region's marketing return on investment. The policy maps ROI ranges to score tiers, and every store in a given region receives that region's marketing efficiency score.

Each store's composite closure score is a weighted sum of the four individual factor scores, using the weights documented in the scoring policy. Stores whose composite score meets or exceeds the closure threshold specified in the policy are flagged as closure candidates. In the event of tied composite scores, stores should be ordered by their identifier in ascending lexicographic order.

For every closure candidate, compute the estimated annual cost savings. The annual rent component uses the proposed monthly renewal rent multiplied by twelve. Annual operational costs are derived from the monthly cost figures found in the per-store data files, covering utilities, maintenance, insurance, supplies, and miscellaneous expenses, each multiplied by twelve. Projected annual net revenue equals the most recent twelve-month net revenue total scaled by one plus the revenue trend expressed as a fraction. Estimated savings equals the sum of annual rent and annual operational costs minus the projected annual net revenue, rounded to the nearest whole number.

Save the results as `closure_recommendations.csv` containing the following columns in this exact order: `store_id`, `store_name`, `region`, `closure_priority_rank`, `composite_score`, `revenue_trend_score`, `competitor_pressure_score`, `lease_cost_score`, `marketing_efficiency_score`, `estimated_annual_savings`. Rows must appear sorted by composite score in descending order, with the rank reflecting that ordering starting at one. All score values should be rounded to two decimal places. Estimated annual savings should appear as a whole number.

In your final response, state the total number of stores recommended for closure and the aggregate estimated annual savings across all recommended stores, expressed as a dollar amount.
