Your organization manages a pharmaceutical cold chain distribution network. A batch of shipments has been flagged for review following reports of potential temperature excursions during transit. You must evaluate every flagged shipment, cross-reference all available data sources, and produce a comprehensive disposition roster recommending either release or quarantine for each one.

Begin by identifying all shipments currently marked as pending review using the available query tools. For every such shipment, retrieve its full details to determine which product it carries, which carrier handled it, and which warehouses served as origin and destination.

Next, pull the complete temperature log recorded during each shipment's transit. Compare every reading against the product's documented acceptable temperature range, which you can obtain through the product information tool. Any reading that falls outside the acceptable minimum or maximum constitutes an excursion. When one or more excursion readings are found, calculate the maximum deviation in degrees Celsius, defined as the largest absolute difference between an excursion reading and the nearest boundary of the acceptable range, and format it to two decimal places. Estimate the total excursion duration in minutes using the proportional method: divide the number of excursion readings by the total number of readings, multiply by the monitoring span in minutes from the first to the last temperature reading, and round to the nearest integer. When no excursion is detected, both the deviation and duration are zero.

Product-specific stability data is documented in profile files available in your working directory. These profiles specify, for each product, the maximum temperature deviation and maximum excursion duration it can tolerate without compromising viability. If the observed maximum deviation exceeds the product's allowed deviation or the excursion duration exceeds the allowed duration, the excursion is classified as exceeding stability tolerance.

Carrier certification records are also available in your working directory. These records indicate whether each carrier holds current cold chain certification. When an excursion is detected on a shipment handled by a carrier that is not cold chain certified, the shipment must be quarantined regardless of excursion magnitude.

For each flagged shipment, retrieve any incident reports from the database. Determine the highest severity level among all incidents associated with that shipment, using the scale: none, minor, moderate, major, critical. A shipment with a critical-severity incident must be quarantined if any excursion was detected. Separately, if an excursion was detected and the highest incident severity is moderate or higher, the shipment must be quarantined even when the excursion falls within stability tolerance.

Warehouse thermal mapping studies in your working directory document the compliance status of zones within each facility. If any zone in either the origin or destination warehouse shows a failed compliance status, that warehouse is considered thermally noncompliant. An excursion detected on a shipment that passed through a noncompliant warehouse triggers quarantine.

The disposition policy configuration file in your working directory establishes the priority order for evaluating these rules when an excursion is detected. Evaluate in this sequence: first check carrier certification, then check for critical incidents, then check whether the excursion exceeds stability tolerance, then check warehouse thermal compliance, then check for moderate-or-higher-severity incidents. If none of those conditions triggers quarantine, the excursion is within acceptable tolerance and the shipment may be released. The reason recorded in the disposition roster should reflect whichever rule first triggered the decision. If no excursion was detected at all, the shipment is released with the reason noting that no temperature excursion was detected.

Save the final output as `disposition_roster.csv` in your working directory. The file must contain exactly these columns in this order: `shipment_id`, `product_id`, `product_name`, `excursion_detected`, `max_deviation_celsius`, `excursion_duration_minutes`, `highest_incident_severity`, `carrier_certified`, `disposition`, `reason`. Use lowercase `yes` or `no` for the `excursion_detected` and `carrier_certified` columns. Format `max_deviation_celsius` to two decimal places and `excursion_duration_minutes` as an integer. The `disposition` column contains either `RELEASE` or `QUARANTINE`. Sort all rows by `shipment_id` in ascending order.

In your final response, state the total number of shipments reviewed, how many were recommended for release, and how many were recommended for quarantine.
