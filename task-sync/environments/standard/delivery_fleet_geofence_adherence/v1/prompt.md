A national delivery fleet operation requires a weekly compliance audit covering every depot in the network. The compliance team has assembled geofence violation records, customer service-level agreements, driver rosters, shift logs, and detailed incident context data. Your task is to produce two deliverables that summarize the reporting week of January 6 through January 12, 2025: a per-depot compliance scorecard and a list of drivers flagged for mandatory retraining.

Begin by familiarizing yourself with the available data files. You will find configuration files that describe the scoring methodology, including severity-based weights for each violation category, multipliers that vary by customer contract tier, thresholds that partition depot risk ratings, and the criteria that trigger a driver retraining flag. Incident context records accompany a subset of the violations and supply an adjustment factor that modifies the severity weight for that particular event. Any violation whose incident context record is marked as disputed must be excluded from all calculations entirely. When no incident context record exists for a given violation, the default adjustment factor specified in the scoring configuration applies instead.

Compliance scoring for a depot proceeds as follows. Collect every non-disputed geofence violation committed by drivers assigned to that depot during the reporting week. Each such violation carries a penalty equal to its severity weight, multiplied by the violation weight multiplier for the SLA tier of the customer where the violation occurred, then further multiplied by the adjustment factor. The SLA tier for a given customer is found in that customer's contract details. Once all violation penalties for a depot have been computed, check for SLA breaches: group the non-disputed violations by customer, and whenever a customer's count exceeds the maximum weekly violations permitted under their contract, that customer is in breach. Every violation at a breached customer has its penalty additionally multiplied by the SLA breach multiplier from the scoring configuration. The depot's compliance score equals one hundred minus the sum of all its violation penalties divided by the number of active drivers assigned to that depot, floored at zero and rounded to two decimal places. If a depot has no active drivers, treat the divisor as one.

Once all depot scores are computed, assign a risk rating to each depot according to the risk thresholds in the scoring configuration. A score at or above the low threshold earns a rating of low, a score at or above the medium threshold but below the low threshold earns medium, at or above the high threshold but below medium earns high, and anything below that earns critical.

Separately, compute the SLA breach count for each depot, defined as the number of distinct customers whose non-disputed weekly violation count from that depot's drivers exceeds the contractual maximum.

Save the scorecard as `compliance_scorecard.csv` with the following columns in exactly this order: `depot_id`, `depot_name`, `region`, `total_drivers`, `active_drivers`, `total_violations`, `critical_violations`, `sla_breach_count`, `compliance_score`, `risk_rating`. The `total_drivers` column counts all drivers assigned to the depot regardless of status, `active_drivers` counts only those with active status, `total_violations` is the count of non-disputed violations, and `critical_violations` is the count of non-disputed violations with critical severity. Sort the rows by `depot_id` in ascending order.

For the retraining report, evaluate every driver across the entire fleet. A driver is flagged if any of the following conditions from the retraining policy configuration are met: their total non-disputed violation count is at or above the minimum violations threshold, their weighted severity score is at or above the minimum weighted severity score threshold, or they have at least one non-disputed critical-severity violation when the critical violation auto-flag setting is enabled. The weighted severity score for a driver is the sum of each non-disputed violation's severity weight multiplied by its adjustment factor. The primary flag reason should be `critical_violation` if the driver has any non-disputed critical violations, otherwise `high_severity_score` if their weighted severity score meets the threshold, otherwise `excessive_violations`. The recommended action is determined by comparing the driver's weighted severity score against the action thresholds in the retraining policy: a score strictly above thirty maps to `immediate_suspension_and_retraining`, a score strictly above fifteen maps to `mandatory_retraining`, and anything else maps to `verbal_warning_and_monitoring`.

Save the flagged drivers report as `flagged_drivers_report.csv` with columns in this order: `driver_id`, `driver_name`, `depot_id`, `depot_name`, `violation_count`, `weighted_severity_score`, `primary_flag_reason`, `recommended_action`. Format each driver name as the first name followed by a space and then the last name. Round `weighted_severity_score` to two decimal places. Sort the rows by `weighted_severity_score` in descending order, breaking ties by `driver_id` in ascending order.

In your final response, state the overall fleet compliance score averaged across all depots rounded to two decimal places, report how many depots fall into each risk rating category, and state the total number of drivers flagged for retraining.
