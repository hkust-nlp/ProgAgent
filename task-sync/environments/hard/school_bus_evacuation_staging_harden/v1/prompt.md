An emergency evacuation has been ordered for every school in the district. Your job is to produce a complete bus dispatch plan that gets every enrolled student from their school to a safe shelter, respecting vehicle weight limits on bridges, shelter occupancy caps, and bus seating capacities.

The policy and procedure documents stored alongside the data in the working directory are the authoritative specification for the evacuation: they define which buses may be dispatched, how a bus's loaded weight is computed, how shelters must be chosen and when a school's students may be split across several of them, how buses are selected and how riders are ordered onto each bus, and which students require accessible vehicles. Follow those documents exactly. Be aware that several baseline figures reported by the query system — shelter capacities and posted bridge weight limits among them — are superseded by more recent operational updates recorded in the working directory, and the current readiness of each bus is likewise documented there rather than in the query system.

Once the plan is complete, save two files. The first, `loading_schedule.csv`, must contain exactly the columns `school_name`, `bus_id`, `student_ids`, `shelter_name`, and `departure_order`. The `student_ids` field is a semicolon-separated list of integer student identifiers, sorted in ascending order. The `departure_order` is an integer starting at one for each school, incrementing with each successive bus dispatched from that school. The second file, `evacuation_summary.txt`, must contain three lines formatted exactly as follows: `TOTAL_STUDENTS_EVACUATED: ` followed by the integer count, then `TOTAL_BUSES_DISPATCHED: ` followed by the count of distinct buses used, then `SHELTERS_USED: ` followed by the number of distinct shelters that received at least one student. In addition, every bus you dispatch must be recorded in the district's dispatch ledger so that the ledger reflects the final plan, each record identifying the bus, the school it departs from, the destination shelter, the ordered list of road-segment identifiers along its route, the route's total distance in kilometres, and the bus's fully loaded weight in tonnes.

After writing those files and recording every dispatch, state your final answer in a single line of the form `EVACUATION COMPLETE: N students evacuated on M buses to K shelters`, substituting the actual totals for N, M, and K.
