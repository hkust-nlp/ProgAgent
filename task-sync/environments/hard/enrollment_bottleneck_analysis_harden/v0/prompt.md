You are tasked with analyzing the recruitment performance of a multi-center clinical trial to identify sites experiencing enrollment bottlenecks and produce a prioritized intervention list. Recruitment operations data for the trial's sites — screening outcomes, eligibility assessments, monthly enrollment history, and investigator staffing — is accessible through the available tools, while the governing methodology and configuration documents, along with supplementary per-site records, are stored alongside the data.

Those documents are the sole authoritative specification for this analysis. They define which sites are in scope, how each bottleneck dimension is measured and flagged, how the severity score is assembled, adjusted, capped, and rounded, and which recommended intervention applies to each bottleneck type. Every calculation must follow them exactly, and nothing beyond them should be assumed.

Sort all sites that have at least one bottleneck by severity score in descending order, breaking ties by site identifier in ascending alphabetical order. Assign a priority rank starting at one for the highest severity. Save the results as `site_intervention_priorities.csv` with columns in this exact order: `site_id`, `site_name`, `region`, `bottleneck_type`, `severity_score`, `recommended_intervention`, `priority_rank`.

In your final response, state the total number of active sites analyzed, the number of sites requiring intervention, the top three most common bottleneck types across all flagged sites, and the details of the five highest-priority sites including their site identifiers, names, bottleneck types, severity scores, and ranks.
