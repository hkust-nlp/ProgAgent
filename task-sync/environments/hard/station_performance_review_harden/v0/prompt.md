Your organization operates a shared mobility network spanning several districts, with stations offering bikes, scooters, and e-bikes. Leadership has requested a thorough evaluation of station performance to identify those that should be considered for decommissioning or relocation to areas with higher demand, and expects a definitive ranked list of underperforming stations.

The scoring and criteria documents stored alongside the data are the authoritative specification for this review: they define which stations are eligible for evaluation, the four performance dimensions and their weights, the normalization caps and precise formulas for each component score and for the overall composite score, the threshold a station must meet to qualify as a candidate, and the rules that distinguish a decommission recommendation from a relocation recommendation. Every number in your output must follow those definitions exactly. The station-level records needed to apply them are spread across the remaining workspace documents and the retrieval capabilities available to you; no single source is sufficient on its own, so consult them all when assembling each station's performance picture.

Save the final output as `relocation_candidates.csv` containing the columns `rank`, `station_id`, `station_name`, `district`, `composite_score`, and `recommendation`, in exactly that order. Rows must be sorted by composite score in descending order, with ties in composite score broken by station identifier in ascending order. The rank column should reflect this ordering starting from one. Composite scores must be written with exactly two decimal places. The recommendation column must contain either `DECOMMISSION` or `RELOCATE` in uppercase letters.

Once the file has been written, respond with a summary stating the total number of candidate stations identified, how many are recommended for decommissioning versus relocation, and the station identifier and name of the top five candidates ranked by composite score.
