The communications team needs a comprehensive brand reputation monitoring report that aggregates signals from multiple channels and produces a prioritised alert queue. Every brand under monitoring must be assessed across four signal categories — social media volume anomaly, news headline sentiment, support ticket sentiment, and executive communications risk — and these signals combined into a unified severity score that determines which brands require immediate attention.

The methodology and configuration documents stored alongside the data are the authoritative specification for this analysis: they define the exact formula for each signal, the sentiment lexicon, the historical baselines, the tier assignments, the analyst severity assessments, the executive watchlists, and how the signals are scaled, weighted, combined, and thresholded into the final severity score. Follow them exactly.

Any brand whose final severity score meets or exceeds the alert threshold specified in the priority configuration should be included in the output alert queue. The queue must be sorted in descending order of severity score, with ties broken by ascending brand identifier. Save the alert queue as `alert_queue.csv` with the following columns in this exact order: `rank`, `brand_id`, `brand_name`, `monitoring_tier`, `severity_score`, `social_zscore`, `headline_risk`, `ticket_negativity`, `exec_risk_flag`. The rank column should be an integer starting at one, severity_score and social_zscore should be rounded to two decimal places, headline_risk and ticket_negativity should be rounded to four decimal places, and exec_risk_flag should be zero or one.

In addition, every alert in the queue must be individually filed into the central reputation alert queue of the monitoring platform, recording the alerted brand and its final severity score, so that the platform's alert register reflects exactly the brands in your queue.

In your final response, state the total number of alerts in the format "Total alerts: N" and list all alerted brand names in priority order separated by commas on a line beginning with "Priority order: ".
