You are responsible for producing a dynamic pricing recommendation for a podcast advertising network. The network runs campaigns that place ads into podcast shows across geographic regions, and historical impression data has been accumulating for months. Leadership wants a comprehensive pricing schedule that adjusts each qualifying show-region pair's CPM rate based on how well inventory is actually selling, where the listeners are concentrated, which advertiser category dominates the revenue, and what seasonal demand looks like for the upcoming quarter.

The pricing policy and configuration documents stored alongside the data are the authoritative specification: they define the pricing formula, the adjustment tiers and their multipliers, the seasonal factors and target quarter, which show-region pairs qualify for the schedule, and how edge cases are resolved. Nothing beyond those documents should be assumed, and every calculation must follow them.

Save the output as `pricing_schedule.csv`. It must contain exactly these columns in this order: `show_id`, `show_name`, `region_id`, `region_name`, `current_cpm`, `fill_rate`, `fill_rate_multiplier`, `geo_concentration`, `geo_multiplier`, `category`, `category_factor`, `seasonal_factor`, `recommended_cpm`, `adjustment_pct`. Sort the rows first by show identifier in ascending order, then by region identifier in ascending order. Every qualifying show-region pair must appear exactly once.
