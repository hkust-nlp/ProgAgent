Every quarter the regional environmental compliance division conducts a comprehensive review of all registered industrial facilities to determine which ones pose the greatest risk of violating their discharge regulations. The review weighs current effluent measurements against legally permitted discharge limits, alongside inspection violation history, the maintenance condition of pollution-control equipment, independent laboratory analyses, and the status of corrective action plans filed in response to past violations. Your task is to carry out this assessment and produce a single, prioritized dispatch list that field inspectors will use to schedule their upcoming site visits.

The assessment methodology document and the configuration and reference files stored alongside the data are the authoritative specification: they define which facilities are in scope, how each of the five risk components is computed, the weights, recency factors, and multipliers involved, the thresholds that map scores to risk categories and dispatch urgencies, and how each facility's primary concern is determined. Every figure in your deliverable must follow those definitions exactly — nothing beyond them should be assumed.

Save the complete assessment as a file named `dispatch_list.csv` containing the following columns in this exact order: `rank`, `facility_id`, `facility_name`, `region`, `risk_score`, `risk_category`, `primary_concern`, `dispatch_urgency`. Every Active facility must appear exactly once regardless of its risk level. Round the final risk score to two decimal places. Rows must be sorted by risk score from highest to lowest, with ties broken by facility identifier in ascending lexicographic order, and the rank column must reflect this ordering starting from one.

Once the file has been written, provide a brief narrative summary stating the total number of facilities assessed, the count of facilities falling into each risk category, and the facility identifiers together with names and risk scores of the five highest-risk facilities.
