A natural gas pipeline operator needs to validate and finalize capacity bookings for an upcoming nomination cycle. Shippers have submitted reservation requests across various pipeline segments, and every pending reservation must be evaluated against current operational conditions — scheduled maintenance, weather, flow volatility, and any regulatory constraints — before capacity can be allocated.

The allocation policy and methodology documents stored alongside the reservation data are the authoritative specification for the entire process: which reservations are in scope, how each segment's effective capacity for the nomination date is derived, how reservations are prioritized, how capacity is allocated, and how each outcome is classified. Follow them exactly. Every finalized allocation decision must also be recorded in the operator's booking system so that the database reflects the outcome for each reservation.

The finalized allocation must be saved as `capacity_allocation_report.csv` with the following columns in exactly this order: `segment_id`, `segment_name`, `reservation_id`, `shipper_id`, `shipper_name`, `contract_tier`, `reliability_score`, `priority_flag`, `nominated_volume_mmbtu`, `allocated_capacity_mmbtu`, `allocation_status`, `effective_segment_capacity_mmbtu`. Rows must be sorted by segment identifier ascending, and within each segment by the allocation priority order defined in the methodology. All numeric capacity and volume values must use two decimal places. Reliability scores must retain one decimal place.

After producing the report, provide a summary stating the total number of segments processed, the total number of reservations handled, how many were confirmed, how many were curtailed, how many were rejected, the total nominated volume across all reservations, the total allocated capacity, and the overall utilization rate expressed as the total allocated capacity divided by the sum of effective segment capacities across all segments that had reservations, formatted as a percentage rounded to two decimal places.
